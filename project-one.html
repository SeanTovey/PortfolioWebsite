Dream Parasite

VR Unity Game (URP)

Date: 2026 - Ongoing

Timeline: Four Months (cont.)

Collaborators: Dineth Meehitiya, Huy Lam Duc, Rohan Jose, and Quan Au

Tools: Blender, Unity, Unity Shader Graph, C#

Dream Parasite is a surreal VR experience where you travel through strange dream worlds and dig through them to repair the damaged parts of your own world. As you explore, you uncover different dream environments, collect objects, and navigate through each world until you reach the end and restore what has been broken.

Dineth Meehitiya: 2D Textures, Sound, 3D Modeling
Huy Lam Duc: Terrain Generation
Rohan Jose: VR Controls
Quan Au: Particle VFX
Myself: 3D Modeling, Level Design, Shader VFX
