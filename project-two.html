Lighthouse Keeper

3D Unity Game (URP)

Timeline: Three Months

Tools: Blender, Unity, C#

Lighthouse Keeper is a first person game environment simulating a lighthouse based on historical lighthouses of the east coast of North America. The project explores the sense of isolation and the tedious and mundane activities of someone at the end of the world.

This project was done in my Master’s program over the course of three months and was quite ambitious at the start. I wanted this to be a full fledged game with real time weather systems, day/night cycles, and time based events on a schedule.
