Lighthouse Keeper

3D Unity Game (URP)

Timeline: Three Months

Tools: Blender, Unity, C#

Lighthouse Keeper is a first person game environment simulating a lighthouse based on historical lighthouses of the east coast of North America. The project explores the sense of isolation and the tedious and mundane activities of someone at the end of the world.

This project was done in my Master’s program over the course of three months and was quite ambitious at the start. I wanted this to be a full fledged game with real time weather systems, day/night cycles, and time based events on a schedule.

The bulk of the time creating this project went into creating the hero piece of the lighthouse. Every model was handmade in Blender and based on historical diagrams of specifically Stannard Rock Lighthouse. Because I wanted the flow of the interior and the events that take place within to be as historical as possible, I iterated over the lighthouse design many times throughout the project since each location needed to look good from any angle while being navigable.

I ended up using premade assets to create the day/night cycle and real-time weather system. I used COZY: Stylized Weather 3 for the framework to hook up time and weather based events.

The project is in more of a demo state than a full game, but the environment as an outcome turned out in a great way.

The final result is a playable environment with a day/night cycle, weather system, and time based events. The player can explore the environmentm, interact with the objects within it, such as the light and horn, to stop passing ships from crashing into the rocks.
