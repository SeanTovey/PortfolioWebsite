About me

I am a 3D environment artist focused on stylised real-time game environments. I create dark and surreal environments, playing with color and texture to create otherworldly scenes that evoke the sublime.

I am interested in exploring XR technologies to create unreal worlds that bring the player physically into the digital. I also enjoy using procedural texturing, shaders, and illusions for stylistic and performance-friendly alternatives to hyper-realism that prioritize art direction over physical accuracy. I believe that games should be accessible to everyone no matter their hardware.

I am experienced in working as a team to turn rough concepts into real-time interactive game spaces using Blender, Unity, and Unity Shader Graph.
